The problem

MCP is powerful. Right now it’s also wide open.

The Model Context Protocol lets assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor use your real tools. That means real access, and the ecosystem hasn’t caught up.

Anyone can publish

Thousands of servers, most of them unaudited. One tool description can slip hidden instructions to your AI.

Keys everywhere

API tokens pasted into a dozen config files across Claude, Cursor and your IDE. Every copy is one more to rotate.

Hard to host

Many servers only run on your laptop. Putting them online means OAuth, HTTPS, updates and uptime.

No paper trail

When an agent touches your inbox or your repo, there’s no easy way to see what it actually did.

How it works

Three steps. One URL.

  1. 1

    Pick your servers

    Choose from the vetted catalog and read each scorecard before you switch anything on.

  2. 2

    Connect your accounts once

    Sign in to GitHub, Gmail, Notion and the rest. Keys go into your vault, not your config files.

  3. 3

    Paste one URL

    Add your private endpoint to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or any MCP client.

mcp.json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "privo": {
      "url": "https://you.privo.club/mcp"
    }
  }
}

That’s the whole config.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is MCP?

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor use outside tools and data: your repos, inbox, docs and databases. An MCP server is the bridge to one of those tools.

Who is Privo Club for?

Developers, founders and teams who use AI assistants every day and want them connected to real tools without the security headache. If you’ve ever hesitated before pasting an API key into a config file, it’s for you.

When does it open?

We’re building now and will invite founding members in small groups, starting with the people who join the list first and tell us what they need.

How much will it cost?

Browsing the catalog and scorecards will be free. Membership is planned at roughly the price of a couple of coffees a month, with team plans for companies. Founding members lock in their rate.

What do you do with my email?

We store your email and any survey answers you choose to give, and use them only to invite you and build the club. No ad trackers on this site, no selling, no sharing. Email support@privo.club and we’ll delete it.
